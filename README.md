# 📊 Telco Customer Churn Analysis
Customer churn is a major challenge for subscription-based businesses. This project analyzes real customer data from a telecommunications company to identify the key drivers of churn and estimate its financial impact. The goal is to support data-driven retention strategies by transforming raw customer data into actionable business insights.
## 🎯 Business Objective
Analyze customer churn behavior to identify high-risk segments and estimate the financial impact of customer attrition.

## 🧠 Key Insights
- Customers on **month-to-month contracts** have the highest churn rate.
- **New customers (0–12 months)** are significantly more likely to churn.
- **Fiber optic users** exhibit higher churn compared to other Internet services.
- Customers using **electronic check payments** churn more than those using automatic payment methods.

## 💡 Recommendations
- Promote long-term contracts through incentives.
- Strengthen onboarding for new customers.
- Improve value perception for premium Internet services.
- Encourage automatic payment methods.
